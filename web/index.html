How it works

Watches

Checks the booking page every 30 seconds for a slot that fits your day, time and group size.

Holds

Grabs it instantly, like clicking it yourself, and keeps renewing the 10 minute hold until you check out.

Buzzes you

Sends a push to your phone. Tap it, pick your food, pay with Apple Pay. Done.

Questions

When do new days open for booking?

Does it keep running on its own?

Yes. It checks every 30 seconds until it holds a slot, keeps that hold alive until you check out, then stops. It also stops if your computer sleeps, you close the window, or your login ends. Stop it any time with Ctrl + C.

Do I need to be at my computer to pay?

No. The hold belongs to your account, not your computer. Any device where you're logged in shows it, so you can order and pay on your phone.

I already have a slot. Will the bot replace it?

Probably. You can only hold one slot at a time, so if the bot holds a new one, your old hold most likely gets swapped out. Only run the bot with a day and time window you'd prefer over what you have.

It says “Not logged in”.

The bot's login ended, usually because you logged out somewhere on the site. Repeat step 4 to make a fresh cookie.txt.

It says PHONE_NOT_VERIFIED or TERMS_UPDATE_REQUIRED.

The site wants something from you first. Open it in your browser, do what it asks, then start the bot again.

Is my data sent anywhere?

The bot only talks to the Nhas ordering site, as you, and to ntfy for the push. This website stores nothing about you: your progress and topic stay in your own browser.
